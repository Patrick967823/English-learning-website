📖 Reading Story: The Beauty of Thailand

The Beauty of Thailand

Thailand is a wonderful country in Southeast Asia. It has beautiful beaches, green mountains, and friendly people. Every year, millions of tourists visit Thailand to see its amazing temples, eat delicious food, and enjoy its warm sunshine.

One of the most famous places is Phuket, with its white sand beaches and clear blue water. Bangkok, the capital city, is full of life, markets, and beautiful temples like Wat Arun.

Comprehension Questions:

1. Where is Thailand located?
2. Name two popular places in Thailand.
3. What do tourists like to do in Thailand?
