📚 English Lessons

1. Greetings & Introductions

Vocabulary: Hello, Hi, Good morning, Nice to meet you, Sawasdee krub/ka

Grammar: Present Simple (I am / You are)

Explanation: Use these phrases to start conversations confidently.

2. Food & Ordering

Key Sentences: "I'd like...," "How much is this?" "Can I have the bill please?"

Listening Practice: Watch Thai street food videos in English.

3. Travel & Directions

Learn how to ask for and give directions in English.

Useful Phrases: "Where is the BTS station?" "Turn left / right."
